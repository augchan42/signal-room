# Signal Room art direction

## Production target

Illustrated management screens and offline pre-rendered Blender performance clips. Portrait output: 1080 × 1920. Start with one five-second loop. Blender is an asset-production tool, not an Android runtime dependency.

## Visual reference

Early-1980s Visage / Fade to Grey: restrained theatrical styling, sculpted hair, angular clothing, deliberate poses, deep shadows and sparse broadcast graphics. Ink blue and cream dominate; electric cyan rim lights and small coral accents. Four stylized adult male performers, with no celebrity likenesses, lip sync, detailed facial animation or cloth simulation in the first pass.

## Music-show setting

The band is booked onto a television music show. The user's references are Top of the Pops and Korean weekly music shows, with Gayo Top 10 as the retro reference. Broadcast reach provides the visual growth arc.

- Tier 1: late-night cable slot, tight stage, one camera, rear screen, no audience.
- Tier 2: mid-week show, additional light frames, fan silhouettes and light sticks, alternating cameras.
- Tier 3: flagship broadcast, expanded lighting rig, larger screen and slow crane-style camera motion.

Reuse the modular stage at every tier. Screen patterns: signal bars, test patterns and waveforms. No modelled host; use captions. Audience representation remains silhouettes and light sticks.

## Band and instruments

Use a band-idol hybrid with four distinct stations in a portrait-friendly diamond:

- Front centre: frontman and microphone, cropped jacket.
- Rear left: producer/leader at a two-tier synthesizer station, long vest. Include an attractive KORG-branded synth. Current concept is a stylized original instrument, not a replica of a specific Korg model.
- Front right: keytar and backing-vocal microphone, oversized blazer.
- Raised rear centre: electronic drum pads, fitted sleeveless top.

Shared body foundation, trousers and boots; four heads/hair silhouettes and four outer garments. Begin with breathing, head turns, weight shifts and limited instrument hand motions. Full choreography is deferred.

## Control-room management UI

The player's seat is a control room overlooking the stage. Five segment monitors represent awareness and affinity; mix-desk faders represent vocal distribution and arrangements; talkback represents the follow-up action; a tape deck/clock represents three schedule slots; an on-air light represents release. The stage clip appears through the window or on a programme monitor.

First validate one static control-room render behind a rough Compose screen, then make tier variants. This is planned work, not part of the current instrument blockout.

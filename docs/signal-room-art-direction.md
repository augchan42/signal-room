# Signal Room art direction

## Production target

Illustrated management screens and offline pre-rendered Blender performance clips. Portrait output: 1080 × 1920. Start with one five-second loop. Blender is an asset-production tool, not an Android runtime dependency.

## Visual reference

Early-1980s Visage / Fade to Grey: restrained theatrical styling, sculpted hair, angular clothing, deliberate poses, deep shadows and sparse broadcast graphics. Ink blue and cream dominate; electric cyan rim lights and small coral accents. Four stylized adult male performers, with no celebrity likenesses, lip sync, detailed facial animation or cloth simulation in the first pass.

## Music-show setting

The band is booked onto a television music show. The user's references are Top of the Pops and Korean weekly music shows, with Gayo Top 10 as the retro reference. Broadcast reach provides the visual growth arc.

- Tier 1: late-night cable slot, tight stage, one camera, rear screen, no audience.
- Tier 2: mid-week show, additional light frames, fan silhouettes and light sticks, alternating cameras.
- Tier 3: flagship broadcast, expanded lighting rig, larger screen and slow crane-style camera motion.

Reuse the modular stage at every tier. Screen patterns: signal bars, test patterns and waveforms. No modelled host; use captions. Audience representation remains silhouettes and light sticks.

## Band and instruments

Use a band-idol hybrid with four distinct stations in a portrait-friendly diamond:

- Front centre: frontman and microphone, cropped jacket.
- Rear left: producer/leader at a two-tier synthesizer station, long vest. Use the invented BORG maker's badge on an original instrument with walnut cheeks, individual keys, knobs and a green display.
- Front right: keytar and backing-vocal microphone, oversized blazer.
- Raised rear centre: electronic drum pads, fitted sleeveless top.

Shared body foundation, trousers and boots; four heads/hair silhouettes and four outer garments. Begin with breathing, head turns, weight shifts and limited instrument hand motions. Full choreography is deferred.

## Control-room management UI

The player's seat is a control room overlooking the stage. Five segment monitors represent awareness and affinity; mix-desk faders represent vocal distribution and arrangements; talkback represents the follow-up action; a tape deck/clock represents three schedule slots; an on-air light represents release. The stage clip appears through the window or on a programme monitor.

The MVP scene includes this booth and three tier renders. The review page prototypes phone UI placement; actual Compose wiring remains Android implementation work.

## Additional approved direction

The five fan segments map to light-stick colours and control-room monitors: performance/cyan, vocal/amber, fashion and concept/pink, story and personality/mint, retro and alternative/violet. A five-weight input changes the colour mix on linked audience geometry. Tier 1 has no audience, tier 2 has a small fan section, and tier 3 expands it.

Portrait output is a member fancam. Four member cameras make stage emphasis visible. A full-group camera and a control-room camera serve other presentation needs. Initial loops are five seconds at 24 fps / 120 BPM, with small weight shifts, breathing, head turns, slow camera drift, beat-pulsing screen brightness and rim lights, and a downbeat on-air pulse. Clips are deliberately readable without sound; no music recording is bundled.

Broadcast finishing adds light chroma displacement, scanlines and a vignette. The late-night tier is inset with stronger CRT distortion; the flagship is clean and full-frame. SIGNAL ROOM is the working show identity; SYNTHWAVE is the working band name. AFTERIMAGE, NIGHT FREQUENCY and LAST SIGNAL are provisional visual hook titles, not completed songs.

The encore state adds a signal-shaped first-win trophy and confetti. A nominee split graphic leads into the first-win reveal; NIGHT INDEX / GLASSHOUSE is a placeholder rival and song label.

Screen patterns are separately exported assets, with occlusion masks and projected screen-corner metadata for app compositing. This avoids multiplying every lighting/camera render by every screen design. Tone presets swap colours on the same garments.

## Special stages and the MOVE reference

Reference: TWICE's Momo, Mina, Dahyun and Chaeyoung covered Taemin's MOVE at Music Bank in Hong Kong on **19 January 2019**, confirmed by [KBS World](https://www.kbsworld.ne.jp/program/detail?prSn=1245). The production interpretation is low-key monochrome styling, one warm face light, silhouette moments, deliberate small movement and held poses. This is a mood and performance-language reference; no song, choreography or likeness is reproduced.

The special-stage preset uses dark matching garments and mirrored/delayed silhouette echoes derived from the actual four performer meshes. Echo delay is one 120-BPM beat (12 frames).

Add **cover stage** to the planned follow-up actions: awareness gain with one or two adjacent segments, high execution risk, and a coherence penalty when styling conflicts with the covered song. Covering a fictional rival's authored hook makes the rival concrete. This is a simulation design decision; the art scene does not implement gameplay scoring.

World-tour stops such as Hong Kong, Berlin and Chile are a deferred tier 4. The MVP remains three studio broadcast tiers.
